# Concept — Ravenclaw / Blue House

Status: brief and these four concept frames approved by the project owner. Archived concept record; see README.md for the final film.

## Three suitable treatments

1. **Ravenclaw Archive — recommended and previewed.** Brand-native midnight surrounds the real browser. Parchment changes feel like turning a page. Bronze lines connect the artwork to the final laptop. The site stays the largest object.
2. **Paper / Ink.** Parchment surrounds the browser throughout, with sharp navy rules. More print-editorial and brighter, less nocturnal.
3. **Midnight / Arena.** Raven blue surrounds the browser with restrained electric-blue punctuation. Quicker camera framing on the four values; more sports campaign energy.

The previews use treatment 1, consistent with the approved brief. Treatments 2 and 3 are alternatives, not three separate films.
