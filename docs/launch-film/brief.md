# RAVENCLAW — ONE HOUSE. ONE BLUE.

Brief approved by the project owner on 1 October 2026. Concept stills subsequently approved; full animation and MP4 rendering authorized.

A 32-second landscape product film of the Ravenclaw / Blue House Sports Day website for Bangpakong "Bowonwittayayon" School. Show the public website's scroll story in a browser window, then reveal the laptop. Let the approved eagle and editorial typography carry the campaign.

## Format and direction

1920 × 1080, 60 fps, 120 BPM, 64 beats / 16 bars. No voiceover. The real website is the visual source of truth. Capture the local production frontend, retaining its fonts, copy, art and layouts. Camera motion and edit timing are deterministic functions of time. All derived materials stay in this folder.

Recommended style: Brand-native / Ravenclaw Archive. Midnight #050A18, parchment #EEE9DC, moonlight #DDE9FF and restrained bronze #A97942. Cormorant Garamond, Geist, Noto Serif Thai and IBM Plex Sans Thai from the site. Slow camera approaches, solid surface changes and scroll-driven reveals; pauses long enough to read the Thai manifesto. No stock footage, invented people or event facts.

## Sound

Restrained instrumental at 120 BPM. Low percussion, sparse metallic accents, little melody over text. Quiet opening, slight lift into Sorting, space for the manifesto, main lift on beat 33 / 16s, then a calm close. Source not selected. The concept is silent. ACE-Step is not installed; installing it or sourcing licensed music requires separate approval. Existing required rendering tools are available.

## Content and assumptions

- The approved static eagle and Blue House marks may appear.
- Film only the identity narrative; exclude personal records, schedules, scores and private admin screens.
- No public website domain has been confirmed. The concept uses localhost in browser chrome and the existing Instagram handle as the follow destination.
- This is a website promo plus a muted loop, not a new video background installed into the website.
- No voiceover, new artwork, publishing, or app changes are part of this brief.
